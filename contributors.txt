OpenPokerDB contributors
========================

This file lists the people who have contributed to the OpenPokerDB fork, which started in 2026.

It deliberately does not carry the upstream fpdb list. The authors of the original project are
credited in each source file's copyright header, and those headers and the AGPL-3.0 license are
unchanged here; the full upstream history is in https://github.com/pikurino/openPokerDB.

An entry is: real or nick name, then eMail, then any other contacts. Everything after the name is
optional. Add yourself in the same format.
